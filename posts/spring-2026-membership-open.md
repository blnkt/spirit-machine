Spirit Machine’s Spring 2026 season is here. Rehearsals are **Thursday evenings, 6:30–8:15 pm** in the Annex Room at **Taborspace** (SE Portland), starting **January 22**. Our performance is **Friday, May 22** at Rose City Park Presbyterian.

No audition—just presence, commitment, and a willingness to work hard and have a great time. You are welcome to try the first rehearsal before committing. See [About](/about/) for membership dues and [Contact](/contact/) for scholarships or questions about matching pitch.
